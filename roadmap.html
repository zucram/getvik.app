Roadmap

Where Vik is going. No dates: things ship when they work.

Updated 4 November 2026. The same list is in the app, under Settings › About › Roadmap.

Coming in 1.0

Being built now, for the first App Store release.

  • One place across every copy PLUSStop the members cut, pick up the YouTube video at the same moment, ads or not.
  • Move from Apple PodcastsBring the shows you follow from a screenshot of your library.
  • Move from SpotifyBring the podcasts you follow from a screenshot of your library, or paste a show's Spotify link.
  • iCloud syncYour library and places on every iPhone you use.
  • CarPlayWaiting for Apple's approval.
  • Resume widgetWhat you were listening to, on the Home Screen.
  • HD videoSharper video on Wi-Fi.
  • Patreon videoVideo posts hosted on Patreon itself, not only YouTube embeds.
  • Skip Silences and Clear Voices PLUSShorter silences and clearer voices.

After 1.0

Decided, coming after the first release.

  • iPadThen, over time, more Apple devices.
  • More languagesVik in your language, not only English.
  • Play before you subscribeListen to an episode from a show's preview without adding the show.
  • Video-only Patreon postsCreators who post video without a podcast episode.
  • Share a clipSend a moment from an episode, audio or video, to a friend or a post.
  • Speed per showEach show remembers its own playback speed.
  • Smart playlistsPlaylists that fill themselves from the shows and rules you choose.

Exploring

Ideas we're looking into. Not promised.

  • Other members feedsPrivate feeds from Supercast, Memberful, Substack and the like, treated as well as Patreon's.
  • YouTube channels as showsFollow a podcast that lives on YouTube but isn't in YouTube Music.

Not planned

Asked about, but not something Vik will do.

  • Spotify playbackSpotify doesn't let other apps play its audio.
  • AudiobooksVik is for podcasts.
  • YouTube downloadsYouTube's rules don't allow it. Podcast feed episodes download as usual.
