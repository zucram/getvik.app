Sources

Do I need YouTube Premium?

To play YouTube episodes in Vik, yes. Vik plays a YouTube episode only when YouTube would play it to your account without ads, and that takes Premium. Without it, Vik tells you so and plays the show's podcast feed instead, when it has one. Podcast feeds and Patreon don't need Premium.

Why does Vik ask me to sign in to Google?

To list the podcasts you follow on YouTube Music, play them on your Premium account, and keep your place in sync with YouTube. You sign in on Google's own page. Vik doesn't read or store your password.

Sign out any time in Settings › Sources › YouTube Music.

What does Vik keep after I sign in, and who sees it?

The sign-in session Google gives the app, the same kind of session a browser keeps. Vik stores it in your iPhone's Keychain, on this phone only (not in iCloud Keychain), and sends it only to YouTube, over an encrypted connection. Vik has no server, so it doesn't go to us or anyone else.

Vik uses the session to read the podcasts you follow, play them, and write your place in each episode to your watch history. That's all it does with it. A session can do more than that, so signing in does mean trusting Vik. If you'd rather not, podcast feeds and Patreon work without a Google sign-in.

Signing out deletes the session and the sign-in page's data from your phone. To end the session at Google too, remove the device in your Google account's security settings.

Which YouTube shows can I follow?

The ones YouTube Music lists as podcasts, including the ones you already follow there. A show that's only an ordinary YouTube channel or playlist doesn't appear yet.

What happens if YouTube changes something?

YouTube episodes may stop playing in Vik until we ship an update, which then goes through Apple's review. Podcast feeds and Patreon don't depend on YouTube and keep working, and a show with a feed copy plays that copy meanwhile.

How do Patreon member feeds work?

Sign in to Patreon in Settings › Sources › Patreon. Vik finds the podcast feeds of the creators you support and adds them to your library with a lock. The feed links Vik finds this way stay in your phone's Keychain and aren't shown in full anywhere in the app.

You don't have to sign in: you can paste the RSS link from your Patreon membership page instead, as in any podcast app. Signing in also brings the YouTube videos creators post with each episode. Video hosted on Patreon itself is still to come.

Can I add a feed that isn't in the directory?

Yes. Paste the feed's link into Search, or use Add a Show at the top of Search. Private feeds from other membership services, such as Supercast or Memberful, work the same way. A private link you add yourself is part of your library, so it syncs to your own private iCloud when sync is on, and it's included when you export your shows.

Can I download episodes?

Episodes from podcast feeds and Patreon, yes, on Wi-Fi only if you like. YouTube episodes stream only: YouTube's rules don't allow saving them. When a show has a feed copy, that's the one to download.

Your place

How does my place reach the TV?

For YouTube episodes, Vik writes your place to your YouTube account as you listen, the same way YouTube's own app does. YouTube on your TV, browser or another phone resumes there. There's no Vik app on the TV; YouTube's own app picks up. When you open Vik again, it reads your account first, so a place you reached elsewhere wins over an older one in Vik.

What if my phone was offline? Will it overwrite a newer place?

No. If saving your place to YouTube fails, Vik keeps it and tries again when you're back online, but first it reads your account. If you've got further somewhere else in the meantime, the older place from your phone is dropped. Playing the same episode on two devices at the same moment is the one case where a place can still get lost.

Why does Vik start a little before where I stopped?

On purpose. Vik goes back about 20 seconds when you resume, so you catch the sentence you were in.

The members cut and the YouTube video are different lengths. Does my place still match?

Yes. Vik works out where the two copies line up: it reads YouTube's captions for the video, transcribes the other copy on your phone, and matches the words. A place in the ad-free cut then becomes the same moment in the version with ads and in the YouTube video. Nothing is sent anywhere to do this.

The free app does this for one show of your choice. With Vik Plus it works for every show.

It needs a few things: YouTube's captions for the video, which can take a while to appear after a new upload; a language your iPhone can transcribe; and Wi-Fi the first time, unless you turn off Wi-Fi Only for Matching Copies in Settings › Sync. Until a match is ready, each copy keeps its own place, so you never land somewhere wrong. Already have a copy downloaded? Then nothing extra is fetched.

Does Vik skip the ads in a show's feed?

No. Ads in a public feed play as they always do. Vik uses where they are only to keep your place between copies. If you want an episode without ads, a members feed is the way to get one.

Moving to Vik

How do I bring my shows from Apple Podcasts or Spotify?

Take screenshots of your library in the other app (up to 30, scrolling between them) and choose them in Vik. Vik reads the show names on your phone, finds each show in Apple's podcast directory, and lets you check the list before anything is added. You can also paste a show's Spotify link.

Your shows come over; your listening history doesn't. Shows that exist only inside Spotify, or that you bought inside Apple Podcasts, have no public feed, so Vik can't find them.

Can I take my shows with me if I leave?

Yes, as an OPML file, which other podcast apps can import: Settings › Import & Export. It holds your feed subscriptions, private feeds included. Shows you follow only through YouTube Music or Patreon sign-in, your places and your bookmarks aren't in it.

Vik Plus

What's in Vik Plus?

One place per episode across every copy and cut, for every show (the free app does it for one), bookmarks that land at the same moment in every copy, Skip Silences and Clear Voices, library folders, shuffle in Up Next, and extra icons and themes. Everything else in Vik is free, and anything free today stays free.

Why a subscription?

Keeping YouTube and Patreon working, and the matching between copies, is ongoing work, not something built once. Plus will be US$14.99 a year from launch (prices in other countries are set by Apple), or US$29.99 a year if you'd like to support Vik, with the same features. The player itself is free, with no ads.

What happens if Plus ends?

Nothing you made is deleted. Your bookmarks stay and still play, and your folders stay. Each copy goes back to keeping its own place, except on your one free show, and Skip Silences, Clear Voices and the extra themes switch off.

How do I cancel or restore my subscription?

Apple handles the subscription. To cancel, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Vik. On a new phone, tap Restore Purchases in Vik's Settings › Vik Plus.

Devices

Which devices does Vik run on?

iPhone, with iOS 27 or later, including the iPhone Duo, where Vik has its own layout for the open screen. iPad comes after 1.0. There's no Android version, and none is planned.

Does Vik work with CarPlay?

Yes. Your shows, Up Next and chapters are on the car's screen.

Privacy

Does Vik track what I listen to?

Vik itself doesn't: no analytics, no ads, no Vik account. The services you use still see their part, as they would in any app: YouTube sees what you play through your account and keeps it in your watch history, and podcast hosts see requests for their files. The privacy policy lists every service Vik talks to.

When something goes wrong

An episode won't play.

Tap Try Again first. If it's a YouTube episode, check Settings › Sources › YouTube Music still says Premium is active. If it keeps failing, send us a diagnostics report (below).

How do I send a diagnostics report?

Settings › Diagnostics › Share. You'll see the report before it goes anywhere, and you choose where it goes. It holds what Vik was doing and any errors; sign-in sessions, private feed links and stream addresses are left out.

Beta (TestFlight) versions can also send diagnostics once a day, if you say yes. The privacy policy explains how. The App Store version never sends them.
