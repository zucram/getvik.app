Private by design

Nothing to sign up for. Nothing to track.

  • No Vik account, no Vik servers.Vik talks straight to the services you already use.
  • Your sign-ins stay on your phone.So do private feed links and what you listen to, plus your own iCloud if you turn on sync.
  • No ads, no analytics.Not in the app, not on this page.
  • Diagnostics only when you send them.You see the report first and choose where it goes.
